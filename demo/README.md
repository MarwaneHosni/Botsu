## Try this

1. Sign up. Add an avatar.
2. Upload a Spanish EPUB. Open it and click a word.
3. Save that word to a deck.
4. Study the deck. Grade a few cards and watch the next review move out.
5. Log a reading, watching, or listening session. Watch XP, level, streak, and badges update.
6. Check the tracker heatmap and the progress page.
7. Look at the leaderboard, then open someone's profile.

## Limits

- **Free tier.** The API sleeps when idle, so the first hit after a while can take a few seconds.
- **No demo account.** Sign up with any new email.
- **No sample book.** Add your own EPUB to try the reader.
- **No source here**, so there's nothing to install. This is the whole thing.
